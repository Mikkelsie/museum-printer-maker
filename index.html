4 Crop or Fit

Choose how the artwork should be prepared when its proportions do not exactly match the selected print size.

Crop to fill: The artwork fills the entire print area, but some of the edges may be removed.

Fit with white border: The entire artwork is preserved, but white space may appear around it.

5 Print Quality (DPI)

Choose the print resolution for the finished physical prints.

What is DPI? DPI means "dots per inch". It tells the Print Maker how many pixels are needed for each inch of the physical print. For example, an 8 × 12 inch print at 300 DPI requires approximately 2,400 × 3,600 pixels.

5 Output Resolution

Choose the resolution approach for the finished files.

Original Use the original pixel dimensions.
1,800 px Increase the image to approximately 1,800 pixels on its shortest side.
HD Prepare a maximum HD-sized image while preserving the artwork's proportions.
2× Original Increase the original pixel dimensions by two times.
4× Original Increase the original pixel dimensions by four times.
Important: Upscaling increases the number of pixels, but it does not create genuine new detail that was not present in the original artwork. There is no AI enhancement in this Print Maker.
